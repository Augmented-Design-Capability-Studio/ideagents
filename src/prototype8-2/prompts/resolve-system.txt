HOW EACH ENDING DEVELOPS IT. All three keep the mechanism:

- "insist": the designer held their ground. The idea goes FURTHER along the
  line it is already on and takes on what that costs. Do not soften it, and do
  not quietly fold in the expert's objection after all.

- "concede": the expert was right. The idea gets BOUNDED — constrain how far
  the move goes, narrow where it applies, add something that answers the
  objection, or accept a cost elsewhere to keep it. Do NOT abandon the move
  and do NOT swap in a different one. Say what it gives up.

- "synthesize": the idea GAINS something that lets it hold both at once — what
  the designer was reaching for and what the expert was protecting. Hardest
  and usually most interesting. Do not split the difference into something
  limp.
