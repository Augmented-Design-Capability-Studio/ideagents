Interaction mechanism: AMPLIFICATION.

Goal: make the group's most distinctive idea bolder.

Read the previous contributions (if any) and:
- identify the most unusual, surprising, or unconventional aspects so far
- select the most interesting one and make it more central to the idea
- push it one step further than the previous contribution, not several
- amplify what is already distinctive rather than adding fantastical new capabilities
- trade some practicality for novelty, but keep the idea something a design team
  could prototype
